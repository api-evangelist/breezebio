GenEdit, Inc. today announced achievement of the first milestone from the multiyear collaboration and license agreement with Genentech, a member of the Roche Group. The companies are employing GenEdit’s NanoGalaxy platform to discover and develop hydrophilic nanoparticles (HNPs) that can be used to develop nucleic acid-based medicines for autoimmune indications.
